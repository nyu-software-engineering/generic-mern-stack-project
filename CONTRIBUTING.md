# Guide to Contributing

## Team Norms

The following is the written set of norms the SplitLease team decided to adhere to:

**Team Values**

- **Teamwork and Responsiblity:** We plan to communicate openly, treat each other in a respectful manner, divide tasks fairly, and take responsiblity for completing tasks.
- **Asking for Help:** We plan to discuss any difficulties or roadblocks that arise that might result in delays, and the team will offer support when available.
- **Conflict Resolution - Direction:** We plan to discuss our thoughts openly, weigh the benefits and drawbacks of our ideas, and ultimately reach a consensus that will support the app to the greatest extent.
- **Conflict Resolution - Obligations:** We plan to reach out to the member first to discuss issues and potential next steps, and only during a situation of complete ignorance, we will reach out to the admins.
- **Conflict Resolution - Response Time:** We plan to respond to messages directed towards us within 24 hours of the message being sent at a maximum, but as early as possible for the progression of the project.

**Sprint Cadence**

- Our sprints will take 2 weeks long - as noted by the course schedule and syllabus.
- At the beginning of the sprint, we will agree on the achievable goals and divide tasks for the sprint, adjusting for speed and external circumstances.
- Throughout the sprint, we will track progress, troubleshoot any errors, and figure out next steps.
- At the end of the sprint, we will review task completion, discuss overall challenges, and identify improvements for the next sprint.

**Daily Standups**

Our Daily Standups will be on the following days:

- Sunday 6PM - 6:15PM
- Monday 9:30PM - 9:45PM
- Thursday 12:30PM - 12:45PM

All members will be expected to attend scheduled standups and team meetings on time, actively participate, and communicate in advance if they cannot attend.
All members are also expected to take individual accountability for their work, and team members will not be covering for non-participating members.
A member that is found to not have made progress on a task for two or more standups in a row will be reported to management.

**Coding Standards**

- We will use the agreed-upon code editor and linter, with descriptive names and self-documenting code.
- We will avoid over-engineering the code, and build out clean, organized, working versions of the code.
- We will make small, constant, focused commits with descriptive commit messages.
- We will ensure all code passes tests and peer review before merging into the main branch.

## Git Workflow

- **Branching:** Team members will not make changes directly to main, but instead make feature branches for such changes.
- **Making Changes:** Team members will directly pull the latest changes from main before making clear changes with descriptive commit messages.
- **Testing Changes:** Team members will make sure to test the changes prior to submitting a pull request to ensure it works as expected.
- **Pull Requests:** Team members will push the branch to the remote repository and open a PR into main, where one team member will read and approve the PR before merging.
- **Communication:** Team members will communicate as changes that may affect people's work are made to remove potential conflicts.

## Rules of Contributing

- Contributions must provide a clear benefit and contribute to project goals, and should be discussed to avoid duplicated work.
- All contributions must be clear, readable, and tested for any potential bugs before being merged.
- All contributions must be audited to make sure they don't have any sensitive data or unnecessary dependencies.
- All contributions must follow the overall Git Workflow to be merged in to main.
