# Flier - Fly through the clutter.

Ever walk into a building and find yourself met with a wall of flyers? It's messy, overwhelming, and for organizations looking to advertise, it adds a level of unnecessary competition amongst communities.

**Flier** is a web application that will provide NYU organizations the chance to share their flyers equitably for students to view and commit to comfortably. 

# Product Vision

For NYU students and student organizations, Flier is a web application that makes discovering and promoting campus events easier, more accessible, and more equitable. Unlike NYU Engage and traditional paper flyers, Flier provides a simple centralized platform where organizations can share events and students can discover, save, follow, and plan for events that fit their interests and schedules.

# Ideation - How did we get here?

When thinking of problems that could be solved by a web-app, Dan thought back to seeing the wall in NYU's Bobst Library that is always cluttered with club flyers. Tons of clubs "overwrite" others and it's hard to intentionally gain traction when there's so much competition. Flier was born from this problem, seeking to reduce friction for club administrators and students, while also (hopefully) contributing to less paper waste!

App name origin: *“Flier” is an acceptable spelling of flyer, which both mean: a small piece of paper containing an advertisement or information that is handed out or posted in public. Flier can also refer to someone or something that flies. We’d like to see all organizations thrive and reach new heights- fly through the clutter. Thus, we adopt the name “Flier.”*
